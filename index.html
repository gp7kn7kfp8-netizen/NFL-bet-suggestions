New to betting? Tap for plain-English meanings
  • To Win the Game: you simply pick the winning team (called the moneyline).
  • To win by X or more points: the favourite has to win by at least that margin. Bookmakers call this the spread or handicap.
  • Win, or lose by fewer than X: a cushion for the underdog. It wins if they win or only narrowly lose.
  • Over X total points: both teams' scores added together must go over that number.
  • To score over X points: that one team must score more than X.
  • Yards (passing, rushing, receiving): a player must gain more than that many yards in the game.
  • Anytime Touchdown Scorer: the player scores a touchdown at any point in the game.
  • Acca / multiple: several bets joined together. Every leg must win, but the odds multiply.
